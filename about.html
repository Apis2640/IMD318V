ABOUT ME

My name is Hafiz Zulkifli. I am the second child in a family of two siblings. I would describe myself as a kind-hearted and helpful person who enjoys supporting others whenever possible. I am also a forgiving person, and I prefer to maintain good relationships with the people around me. I have many friends because I enjoy socialising and spending time with others.

My favourite foods are nasi kandar and nasi kerabu , and my favourite drink is sirap. I also have a strong love for cats, as I find them calm and comforting animals. Football is one of my biggest interests, and I am a supporter of Liverpool. When I play football, I usually take the role of a defensive midfielder (DMF/CDM), where I focus on defending, reading the game, and helping my team control the midfield.

What excites me most is the opportunity to showcase my world in a positive, honest, and captivating way — turning everyday inspiration into something meaningful.
